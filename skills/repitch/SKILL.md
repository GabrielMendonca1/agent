# repitch

Wait. I do not follow where you got to.

Pitch that again:

- Start with the context I was missing — what you were doing and why this came up.
- Plain, simple English. Short sentences, one idea each. No jargon that has not been grounded first; if a term is load-bearing, define it in the same breath.
- Keep the vocabulary of this project and of my vault. Do not invent new names for things that already have one.
- Do not defend the previous message and do not repeat it louder. Rebuild it from the ground.
- End with what you want from me: a decision, a confirmation, or nothing.

This works after the fact. The upfront cure is `grill`: shared language agreed early is what stops the jargon from arriving at all.
